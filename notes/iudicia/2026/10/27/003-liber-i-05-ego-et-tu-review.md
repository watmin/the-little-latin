# Review: Liber I Caput V "Ego et Tū" (2026-10-27)

Size: 9 groups (g-01 to g-09), 119 Q|A frames, a 19-line Lectiō, 4 Law/summary boxes, 1 term box, 21 vocabulary entries, a 6-item drill, 6 homework checkboxes. 40 cards.

## L1 (blocks)
1. Lectiō last line had *Marcus, tū male cantās!* Direct address to Marcus needs the vocative *Marce*, which isn't taught until Ch9. Rewritten as *Sed tū male cantās!* ("But *you* (to Marcus) sing badly!"). *Iōannēs* and *Iūlia* addressed directly are fine (vocative = nominative; STATE rule for Iōannēs).
2. g-07 "Today I eat an apple" produced/discussed *edō*-family first person before the 3rd conjugation is taught (Ch6). Replaced with *Hodiē māla amō* plus a pointer that *edere* waits for Ch6.

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. Pronunciation respellings mixed macron caps (ha-BĒ-mus, KAN-tō) with the house style of earlier chapters (ha-BAY-mus, KAN-toh, kor-NAY-li-am). Normalized throughout.
2. Intro joked "all its moods": "mood" is an untaught grammar term. Now "all six persons".
3. g-09 claimed a Church-Latin "EH-gō with a long o"; not a reliable claim. Removed.
4. g-03 "Every Roman schoolboy knew Laocoön's line": unverifiable overclaim. Now "It's Laocoön's warning about the wooden horse."
5. Lectiō *Nōs nōn rīdēmus / Ego rīdeō* glossed as future ("we won't laugh"). Present tense now glossed as present ("*We're* not laughing" / "*I'm* laughing").
6. g-07 frame "I teach, y'all listen in silence (be quiet)": the English didn't match the Latin (*tacētis*). Now "I teach; y'all keep quiet."
7. Choose card i05-c-sum-acc showed a stem blank (*Discipul___*) with whole-word options. Now *___ sum.* with options *discipulus / discipulum*.

## Checks
- **Accuracy:** 1st conj *amō, amās, amat, amāmus, amātis, amant* (ā lost in *amō*, shortened before *-t/-nt*); 2nd conj *habeō* (short e before vowel), *habēs, habet, habēmus, habētis, habent*; *sum, es* (short e), *est, sumus, estis, sunt*. Macrons checked: *labōrō, cōgitō, respondeō, hīc, hodiē, quoque, nōs, vōs, tū, ego* (short final o, L&S *ĕgo*). Mottos: *Dum spīrō, spērō* (proverb); *Amō, amās, amat* (classroom chant); *Timeō Danaōs et dōna ferentēs* (Virgil, Aen. 2.49); *Homō sum* (Terence, Heaut. 77); *Ōdī et amō* (Catullus 85); *Et tū, Brūte?* (correctly attributed to Shakespeare, flagged as not Roman); *Cōgitō, ergō sum* (Descartes); *Audī, vidē, tacē* (medieval proverb); *Saepe stilum vertās* (Horace, Sat. 1.10.72); *Verba volant…*, *Dīcendō discimus*, *Gutta cavat lapidem* as in prior chapters. No duplication of the auto Tabellae motto.
- **Logic:** -ō induced from *cantō* and tied back to Ch1 chunks (*intellegō, agō*); -s from *agis*; -tis tied to Ch1 *-te / agitis*; person and number defined plainly before use; family vowel induced from *amō/habeō*; *sum* endings compared with the regular set; predicate nominative tied to Ch2 *Mālum est*; pronoun = emphasis induced by contrast.
- **Ordering:** only nominative + accusative; 3rd-conj (*edit, bibit*) kept to 3rd person, Ch6 named; *agis/agitis/intellegō* used as recognition of endings only; no vocative of -us nouns; he/she pronouns deferred; infinitive appears only as the dictionary label (*-āre/-ēre*), not as a construction. *Ōdī* (perfect) and *ferentēs* (participle) appear only in mottos, flagged as later.
- **Structure:** every Q|A table has a marker (g-01…g-09, new chapter, none renumbered); every ## has a motto; dialogue block; Law boxes; nav + Liber I row ✅; home page untouched; `mkdocs build --strict` passes; homework is `- [ ]`; no "cover by hand".
- **Usefulness:** first chapter where John can talk about himself; self-introduction + daily narration drill; Q→A person-swapping (*-s → -ō*, *-tis → -mus*) is the core conversational move.
- **Cards:** 40 cards: 21 vocab (every Vocābula row), 11 forms, 4 choose, 4 error. Build checks C1–C4 pass. Manual: nothing beyond Ch5 (no 3rd-conj persons, no vocative); macrons checked; every choose card has a one-line why; each Law covered (Six Endings: f-endings, e-ego-cantat, e-nos-laborant; Family Vowel: f-habeo-six, e-habeo-macron, f-family; Silent Pronoun: f-i-sing-you-work; sum: f-sum-six, c-es-est, e-tu-sum, c-sum-acc).

## L3 (logged)
- 119 frames is above the rubric's ~40–80 guide (Ch4 was similar). Consider splitting future chapters or trimming g-09.
- *Nunc cantāmus* glossed "Now we sing": a Roman might use the hortatory *cantēmus* ("let's sing"); revisit in Book V.
- *ēs* ("you eat," from *edō*) vs *es* ("you are") would be a fun macron card once *edō* is taught.
- Book II reorder proposals (simple *ubi/postquam/dum*, *sī* + indicative, *dīcit/putō* + acc.-inf. chunks) not applied; Ch5 doesn't conflict with them. Plan files unchanged.
- Ch1 Vocābula already lists *tū*; Ch5 carries it again in *tū · vōs*. Harmless, different card ids.
