## L1 (blocks)
1. Motto 1 had *Esse* without its macron. In *Ēsse oportet ut vīvās, nōn vīvere ut edās* (Rhet. Her. 4.39) *ēsse* is the infinitive of *edō*, "to eat," with a long ē; *esse* (short) is "to be," which flips the sense. Fixed, and the g-02 *ēst* frame now points out that *ēsse* is the same verb.

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. *Ede, bibe, lūde* was glossed "a favorite line on Roman tombstones." Not verifiable: the Latin is a later motto translating the Greek epitaph of Sardanapalus (Strabo 14.5.9; Erasmus, Adagia III.7.27 gives *ede, bibe, lude*). Gloss now: "old Latin motto, after the Greek epitaph of the Assyrian king Sardanapalus."
2. *Canis canīnam nōn ēst*: verified in Varro, *Dē Linguā Latīnā* 7.31 (quoted twice there as an *adagiō*). Citation added. *ēst* with long ē is correct: the athematic 3rd sg of *edō* (L&S *edo*: *ēs, ēst, ēstis*). Gloss tightened to "A dog doesn't eat dog." The g-02 frame calling *ēst* "an old short form" was reworded to "an old, shorter way to say *edit*" (it is a separate old form, not an abbreviation).
3. Section 7 motto duplicated *Ūsus magister est optimus*, which the auto-generated chapter Tabellae section also uses on the same page. Replaced with *Age quod agis* ("Do what you're doing").
4. Ch1-3 cards coverage gaps (from the v1 build self-review) logged to STATE next steps.
