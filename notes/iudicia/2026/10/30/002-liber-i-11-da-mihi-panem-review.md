## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. g-07 garum frame offered "*Amābō vōs…*" as an alternative: *vōs* as an object is untaught (Ch21) and the plural is not the usual idiom. Removed; the answer is just *Date mihi garum, quaesō!*
2. g-05 said *mihi, tibi* have "both i's short." The final i is anceps (*mihī, tibī* in early verse and some poets). Now "Say both i's short (poets sometimes stretch the last one)."
3. Homework/cards error item "*Marcus ad Quīntum cāseum dat*" was labelled wrong, but *dare ad aliquem* is classical for sending (e.g. *litterās ad Atticum dare*). Replaced with the clean learner error *Marcus Quīntum cāseum dat* (accusative for the receiver); card `i11-e-quintum` matches.
4. Logic gap: g-06 taught *Pila Marcō est* ("Marcus has a ball") right after Ch10's *Pila Marcī est* ("the ball is Marcus's") with no contrast; John would mix them. Added a frame contrasting the two (whose is it? vs what does he have?) and a card (`i11-f-marci-marco`).

7. Law of the Look-alikes said the genitive sits "right after a thing owned"; Ch10 allows *Marcī liber* for emphasis. Now "beside a thing owned."

## Checks
- **Accuracy:** *dō, dās, dat, damus, datis, dant; dā, date; dare* (short a everywhere except *dās, dā*; Lewis & Short, Allen & Greenough §202); *dabō* (short a) in the motto. Dative sg. *-ae* (1st: *puellae, aviae, agricolae, Iūliae*), *-ō* (2nd: *Quīntō, magistrō, puerō, avō, Marcō, librō, mālō, hortō, Deō*); pl. *-īs* (*puellīs, puerīs, līberīs, amīcīs, hortīs, librīs, mālīs*); dat. = gen. sg. (1st) and = abl. (2nd sg., all pl.); no preposition governs the dative (classical). *mihi, tibi, nōbīs, vōbīs; mē, tē* (long ē). Dative of possession (*Liber mihi est, Pila Marcō est, Crustula nōbīs sunt, Mihi cāseus nōn est*; A&G §373) and dative of advantage (*Cēnam tibi parō*). *Grātiās* = acc. pl. of *grātia* (long ā); *grātīs* < *grātiīs* (abl. pl., "for thanks only"). *Quaesō* "I ask, beg" (old form beside *quaerō*). *Amābō tē* "please" (Plautus passim; Cicero *Att.* 2.2.1 *Cūrā, amābō tē, Cicerōnem nostrum*). *Pānis, pānis* m., acc. *pānem* (3rd decl.); *pācem* (3rd). Vocab macrons: *grātia, mappa, patella, olīva* (o short, ī long), *cibus* (short i), *dōnum, garum* (short a), *crās*. Table: 5 nouns × 6 cases × 2 numbers re-checked against Ch10's table; only the dative rows are new.
- **Mottos:** *Dā mihi factum, dabō tibi iūs* (civil-law maxim); *Dō ut dēs* (Roman law formula for innominate contracts, *Digest* 19.5.5); *Aviae crustula damus* (own); *Nōn nōbīs, Domine, nōn nōbīs* (Ps. 115:1 / Vulg. 113:9; Templar motto); *Hodiē mihi, crās tibi* (epitaph/memento mori); *Grātiās agimus tibi* (Glōria); *Pānem nostrum cotīdiānum dā nōbīs hodiē* (Lord's Prayer; Luke 11:3 has *cotīdiē*, the Mass *hodiē*; liturgical spelling *quotīdiānum*); *Deō grātiās* (Mass response); *Dā mihi cāseum!* (own); *Dōnā nōbīs pācem* (end of the Agnus Deī); *Verba volant…, Dīcendō discimus, Gutta cavat lapidem* as before. Catullus 12 (Asinius steals the poet's napkin, *lintea*) is described, not quoted.
- **Logic:** *dā mihi pānem* chunk → *dā/date* → *dō* chant guessed from *amāmus* and corrected (short a) → *Quīntō* receiver (subject/object/receiver sorted) → ablative look-alike resolved by company (preposition) → *ad* + acc. vs dative → named dative + indirect object → *-ae* by analogy, *puellae* triple meaning disambiguated by context → *-īs* plural guessed → Law of the Receiver → *mihi/tibi/nōbīs/vōbīs* → dative of possession induced from *Nōmen mihi est* → *Grātiās tibi agō* parsed with Ch4's *-ās* → politeness → six-case table → Law of the Look-alikes. Payoffs: Ch1 *Nōmen mihi est Iōannēs*, *Quid est nōmen tibi?*, *Grātiās tibi agō*, *quaesō*, *grātiās/grātīs*; Ch1/Ch6 mottos *mē*; Ch7 *-ō/-īs* and *mēcum*; Ch8 *ad*; Ch9 *Pater noster*, *Domine*, vocatives; Ch10 table's "Chapter 11" rows, *Deī → Deō*, *crustula* (Horace), Lectiō's *in stomachīs puerōrum*.
