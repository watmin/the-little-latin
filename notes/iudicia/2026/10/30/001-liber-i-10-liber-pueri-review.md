Choice recorded (STATE asked to pick one): **genitive singular of *-ius* nouns = *-iī*** (*fīliī, Cornēliī, gladiī, studiī*), the regular form of later Latin and of dictionaries/Wheelock. g-05 says so explicitly and notes that Cicero and Vergil wrote *fīlī* ("of the son"), which also equals Ch9's vocative. Reason: it keeps the Law of the Twins exceptionless for masculines (gen. sg. = nom. pl.: *puerī, hortī, fīliī*) and keeps Ch9's *fīlī* = calling only.

## Checks
- **Accuracy:** gen. sg. *-ae* (1st) / *-ī* (2nd: *puerī, Marcī, magistrī, librī, amīcī, Tarentī, mālī, avī, Quīntī, parvī, meī, tuī*); gen. pl. *-ārum* (*puellārum, arāneārum*) / *-ōrum* (*puerōrum, līberōrum, oppidōrum, studiōrum, fīliōrum, hortōrum, mālōrum, librōrum*); stress on the long penult (pu-e-ROH-rum, pu-el-LAH-rum). *-ius*: *fīliī, Cornēliī, gladiī, studiī* (choice above); vocative *Cornēlī* (Lectiō *mī Cornēlī*) kept distinct. *Agricolae* (masc. but 1st decl.). Predicate genitive *Marcī est* "it's Marcus's"; possessive as predicate *Mea est, Meī sunt, Tuus est, Nostrī sunt*, agreeing with the thing owned. *Cuius* (= gen. of *quis*, invariable; KOOY-yus, [ˈkui̯.i̯us]). Genitive with adjective/possessive agreeing: *puerī parvī, amīcī meī, tuī Catullī*. Table: all five nouns × 5 cases × 2 numbers checked; *puer/liber* voc. = nom.; neuter nom. = acc. = voc. Dictionary form nom. + gen. sg. Locative sg. = gen. sg. for 1st/2nd-decl singular town names (*Rōmae, Tarentī*); *domī* flagged separate. Vocabulary/macrons: *capsa, pila* (short i; vs *pīlum*), *pūpa* (long ū, Wiktionary [ˈpuː.pa]), *toga, tunica, vīta, calceus, gladius, ānulus* (long ā), *sacculus, crustulum, studium*, *arānea* (long ā, Wiktionary [aˈraː.ne.a]), *plēnus, Annō Dominī, populī, Deī, Agnus*. Sound check: classical *gn* = [ŋn] (ANG-nus) vs ecclesiastical [ɲ] (Allen, *Vox Latina*). Etymologies: *genitīvus* (< *gignō*, "of birth/origin"), *dēclīnāre*, *capsa* (cylindrical book box), *eau-de-vie* calques *aqua vītae*.
