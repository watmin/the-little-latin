## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. g-08 answered "Where is the cheese?" with *In stomachō Marcī*, a genitive (Ch10). Prerequisite violation. Now "In Marcus's stomach. Case closed." with no Latin genitive.
2. Card `i07-c-a-foro` ("from the forum": ā / ab / ē) had more than one right answer: *ab* before a consonant is attested (if less usual), and *ē forō* ("out of the forum") is good Latin too. Replaced by `i07-c-ab-oppido` ("away from the town": *ab* / *ā*), where only *ab* is correct (vowel follows).

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. Section intro blurbs used untaught words (*eadem, aliud, fōrma, ūna/ūnus, multae/multī, littera*). Rewritten with known words only: *Mēnsa in culīnā est. Cāseus in mēnsā est.* · *Mēnsa, mēnsae. In mēnsā… in mēnsīs!* · *Ubi est Marcus? In culīnā. Unde venit? Ē culīnā.*
2. g-08 *Ā forō veniō* frame claimed *ā* is required before a consonant ("Why ā and not ab? Forō starts with a consonant"), contradicting the Law ("ex and ab can go anywhere"). Now: *ā* is the usual form before a consonant; before a vowel it must be *ab*.
3. The ablative definition implied it is just "the case after these five words." Added "for now … it picks up more jobs in later books" (means, time, separation, absolute, etc. come later).
4. The Law of the Long ā said "after *in* (and a few other little words)": vague. Now names *ex, ab, cum, sine* as coming up.
5. Vocabulary domain "house" without *domus*, the commonest word for a (town) house. *Domus* is irregular (4th/2nd mixed, locative *domī* in Ch8), so it's not taught here; g-02 now flags it as a rule-breaker waiting for Chapter 8, consistent with the plan.

## Checks
- **Accuracy:** 1st decl abl. *-ā* (long; contrasts with nom. short *-a*: *mēnsa / mēnsā*), pl. *-īs*; 2nd decl abl. *-ō*, pl. *-īs* (masc. *hortō/hortīs*, neut. *forō/forīs*, *-er* *librō/librīs*). Table in the Law of *-īs* (nom/acc/abl, sg/pl: *mēnsa mēnsae / mēnsam mēnsās / mēnsā mēnsīs*; *hortus hortī / hortum hortōs / hortō hortīs*; *forum fora / forum fora / forō forīs*) verified. Prepositions: *in* + abl. = in/on (rest); *ex/ē* out of; *ab/ā* away from; *ē, ā* only before consonants, *ex, ab* before vowels and *h* (and also often before consonants); *cum, sine*. *mēcum, tēcum* (enclitic *cum*) flagged as chunks. Adjective agreement in the ablative (*in vīllā magnā, in pōculīs magnīs, in parvō*). Macrons checked: *culīna, vīlla, ātrium, cubiculum, taberna, via/viā/viīs, hortus, forum, oppidum, amīcus, stomachus, ubi, ibi, unde, fortasse, ē, ā, mēcum, tēcum, pecūniā, cūrā, līneā, ōvō, māla, vēritās, Americā, silentiō*. Pronunciation: v = w (WI-a, WEEL-lah; church VEE-a, VIL-la flagged); stress *ku-LEE-nah*, *ku-BI-ku-loh*, *AH-tri-oh*.
- **Mottos:** *Ubi tū es?* (simple Latin); *Nūlla diēs sine līneā* (after Pliny NH 35.84, Apelles; already the Ch1/Ch3 homework motto, now decoded); *Multum in parvō* (Ch3 motto, decoded); *In vīnō vēritās* (proverb; *vēritās* flagged as Book II); *Ab ōvō usque ad māla* (Horace, Sat. 1.3.6–7; *ad māla* flagged as Ch8); *Unde venīs?*; *Festīnā lentē, sed nōn sine cāseō* (playful twist on Ch6's motto); *Ubi est cāseus?*; *Sapientia in silentiō* (simple Latin, unattributed); *Verba volant…, Dīcendō discimus, Gutta cavat lapidem* as before. No clash with the auto Tabellae motto.
- **Logic:** lead-in from Ch6 *hūc* vs Ch5 *hīc* → *ubi*; *mēnsa/mēnsā* noticed, then induced across *culīna, vīlla, sella*; *-ō* induced from *hortus, forum, oppidum, pōculum, cubiculum, liber*; plural *-īs* induced from both families; *ex/ab* added with the same ending; *in* vs *ex* contrast (same case, preposition decides); *cum/sine*; summary Law of the Little Words. Payoffs: two old mottos (*sine līneā*, *in parvō*) decoded, *ex librīs*, *ab ōvō usque ad māla* (dinner start to finish), and the Lectiō punch line *In stomachō est!*
- **Ordering:** no genitive (after L1 fix), no vocative of *-us* nouns (Marcus never addressed; *Iōannēs, Iūlia, Cornēlia* = nominative forms), no locative, no city-name ablatives without preposition, no *in* + acc. (motion "into" explicitly deferred to Ch8 *Quō īs?*, as the plan places it), *domus* deferred. *mēcum/tēcum* chunks; *vēritās* (3rd decl) and *ad māla* only in mottos, flagged.
- **Structure:** every Q|A table has a marker (g-01…g-09; new chapter); every ## has a motto; dialogue block (18 lines); Law boxes; nav + Liber I row ✅; home page untouched; `mkdocs build --strict` passes; homework is `- [ ]`; no "cover by hand".
- **Usefulness:** *Ubi est…? In … est. Unde venīs? Ex/Ā … veniō. Venī mēcum!* are core conversational chunks; the drill walks John through his own house; real-text constructions (*ex, ab, cum, in* + abl.) are among the most frequent in any author.
