## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Homework asked for an untaught construction:** the fix-it item gave *Cāseus ēsūrus est* "for 'the cheese is about to be eaten'". The only right answer needs a passive future construction the chapter explicitly defers (g-12 says "a different construction, later"). → the target is now "the cheese has been eaten" (*Cāseus ēsus est*, Ch31), which drills the *ēsus / ēsūrus* trap with known forms.

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **False back-reference (g-07):** "the Helvetians (Chapter 33)": Ch33's text never mentions them; they came in Ch35 (*B.G.* 1.1 *Helvētiī… virtūte praecēdunt*). → "(Chapter 35)".
2. **Unsupported claim (g-10):** "a sea battle on it with condemned men": Suetonius doesn't say so (only modern scholarship and Tacitus describe the fighters). → "the fighters shouted this."
3. **Spelling claim (g-10):** "Suetonius's text spells it *Have*" claims knowledge of the original; editions vary (*Have / Haue*). → "Many editions of Suetonius print *Have*."
4. **Ordering (Lectiō):** Grandpa's *Tōtam noctem vigilātūrus sum* uses accusative of extent of time (Ch45). → *Vigilātūrus sum!* "I'm going to stay awake!"
5. **Overstatement (g-01):** "*Laudāns* and *laudātūrus* differ only in time": they also decline differently (one shape vs *-us, -a, -um*), which the very next frame teaches. → "In meaning, … differ only in time."
6. **Muddled explanation (g-06):** *Cāseus cāsūrus est* was explained as "something about to happen to a thing, but … active is fine," inviting the passive confusion g-12 warns against. → "Falling is something the cheese does itself, so the active *-ūrus* fits. (*cāsum* + *-ūrus*)."
7. **Untaught form unflagged (g-11):** Horace's *mementō* is a future imperative (Ch47). → glossed as "remember, a chunk whose form waits for Chapter 47."
8. **Weak motto (Vocābula):** *Verba discere incipiēmus* didn't use the chapter's grammar. → *Verba lēctūrī sumus*, "We're about to read the words."
9. **Draft Tabellae section** had no cards. → deck appended to the draft.
