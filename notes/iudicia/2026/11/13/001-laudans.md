## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Keep-out broken (Homework motto):** *Faciendō discimus* is a gerund (Liber V). → *Labōrantēs discimus*, "We learn by working."
2. **False sound rule (g-02):** "a vowel before *nt* is always short." Not true in general (*nūntius*, *contiō*-type words). → "before *ns* always long; before *nt* almost always short, and in these participles always."
3. **Contradicted its own Law (g-09):** *parēns* was offered as the participle of *-iō* verb *pariō*, against the Law of -ns and -nt (*-iō → -iēns*). → the frame now says the working participle is *pariēns* (as the Law predicts), and *parēns* is an older form kept as a noun; *pariēns* added to the glossed list.
