Decisions recorded:
- New terms, each in plain words: **voice** ("whether the subject does the action or receives it"), **active**, **passive** (*passīvus*, "putting up with, having it done to you"; English "is loved / is being eaten"), **agent** ("the doer in a passive sentence"; *agō*), **ablative of agent** (*ā/ab* + doer), **ablative of means** (plain ablative of the tool; old-school "ablative of instrument"). *Deponent* named once for recognition only (Ch43).
- Laws: Passive Endings (*-r, -ris, -tur, -mur, -minī, -ntur*; vowel short before final *-r*); Doer and the Tool (doer → *ā/ab*; tool → plain abl.; company → *cum*, Ch7); Passive Clock (time signs unchanged; twists *-beris* and *legeris/legēris*).
- Sound rule taught as a reading aid: short *i* → *e* before *r* (*legeris, caperis, amāberis*), tied to *leg-e-re*.
- Payoffs: Ch4 *Amor* (noun = verb "I am loved": the title pun), Ch3 *Nōn semper ea sunt quae videntur* and Ch18 *Possunt, quia posse videntur* (*videor* = "be seen" = "seem"), Ch16 *amābō tē* → *amābor*, Ch28 *servō* (*Servāmur!*), Ch29 *quō* → *Ā quō?*, Ch2 motto *Quaerite et inveniētis* inside Matt. 7:7, Ch23 *manū*/*fidē*, Ch15 Caesar 1.1 continued, Ch25 *Factum est* and *lēgī*, Ch21 *sē lavat* vs *lavātur*, Ch14 dog named Cāseus (*Cāseus ā Cāseō editur*).
- Real-author anchors (checked against texts): Vergil *Ecl.* 10.69; Horace *S.* 1.2.11 *laudātur ab hīs, culpātur ab illīs* (the spendthrift); Caesar *B.G.* 1.1 *quī ipsōrum linguā Celtae, nostrā Gallī appellantur*; *Sōlā fidē* (Reformation slogan); Phaedrus 4.2.5; Vergil *A.* 5.231; Publilius Syrus A22 *Amāre et sapere vix deō concēditur*; Mark 1:5 Vulg. *baptīzābantur ab illō*; Matt. 7:7 Vulg. *Petite, et dabitur vōbīs…aperiētur vōbīs*; Augustine *Conf.* 3.1.1 *amāre et amārī dulce mihi erat* (word order checked: *dulce mihi*); *Mundus vult dēcipī* (16th-c. proverb, recorded by Sebastian Franck; not Petronius); *Tempora mūtantur, nōs et mūtāmur in illīs* (16th-c. proverb, not Ovid).
