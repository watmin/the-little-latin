Three ways to pass on speech: statement (Ch42, acc. + inf.), question (Ch53), command (today).
- Person asked by verb: dative *imperō, persuādeō* (Ch44); accusative *rogō, ōrō, hortor, moneō*; *ā/ab* + ablative *petō, postulō*. *Nē* not *ut nōn* (Ch52 Two Nots); *nē quis/quid/cui/quem* (Ch51 paid); *nēve/neu* "and not" carded.
- Matching rule: only present/imperfect in commands ("orders point forward"); "have done" perfect → now team (Ch54); passive orders (*portētur/portārētur*).
- *Iubeō/vetō* + accusative + infinitive (A&G §563a), "normally"; passive infinitive with *iubeō* (*BG* 1.7.2). Ch19 *iubeō*, Ch48 *togam induere iussērunt*, Ch38 Horace *quid vetat* paid.
- "That" vs "to": *persuādeō, moneō, dīcō* + acc. + inf. vs *ut/nē* (Matthew 4:3 *dīc ut*).
- Ch50 *velim mihi scrībās* paid: *ut* omitted after *velim/volō* (A&G §565). *Pro Quinctio* 19 direct *nunc hoc velim cūrēs … quod dīxistī* paired with 18 reported *rogat ut cūret quod dīxisset*; mixed sequence after historic present (A&G §485e quotes this line). Ch54's cut paid.
