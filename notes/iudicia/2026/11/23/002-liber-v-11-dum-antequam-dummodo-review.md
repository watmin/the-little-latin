Decisions recorded:
- Terms (plain + old-school): **temporal clause** = "the time clause"; **anticipatory subjunctive** = "the waiting subjunctive" (A&G §550–551, §553); **proviso clause** = "the provided-that clause" (old-school *clause of proviso*, A&G §528); **concessive clause** = "the although clause" (Ch55 named concessive *cum*; A&G §527); **relative clause of characteristic** = "the kind-of clause" (A&G §534–535).
- Four *dum*s: while + present indicative even in past narrative (A&G §556); as long as + same tense (§555; Lectiō *Dum vīvam … dabō*); until + subjunctive after waiting (§553; *dum* + indicative "until" called rare, *dōnec* named only); provided that + subjunctive, negative *nē* (§528).
- *antequam/priusquam*: perfect indicative = fact (§551a); imperfect/present subjunctive = expected/headed off (§551b, note 2 for historic present); future "before" = present indicative (§551c). Matthew 26:34 *antequam gallus cantet* flagged as Bible Latin's subjunctive where classical prose would usually say the indicative.
- *postquam, ubi, simul atque* + perfect indicative (§543), tied to Ch55 *cum prīmum*; *post* vs *postquam*.
- *quamquam* + ind. (fact), sentence-initial = "and yet" (§527d and note); *quamvīs* + subj.; *licet* + present/perfect subj. (§527b); poets blur (§527e).
- Characteristic: *sunt quī, nēmō est quī, quis est quī, is quī, tam … quī* (§535a, §534 tie to result = Ch52); *quīn* = *quī nōn* after a negative (§559).
- Paid: Ch5 *Dum spīrō, spērō*; Ch49 *Gaudeāmus … dum sumus*; Ch43 Lectiō *vescēbātur dum Pater cantābat*; Ch27 *dōnec*; Ch46 *ōdī* (→ *ōderint*); Ch44 *licet*; Ch18 *vīs*; Ch38 *tamen*; Ch51 *Puer quī cantat*; Ch52 *tam … ut*; Ch55 concessive *cum*, *cum prīmum*; Ch58 *quīn* and BG 1.19.2 (Ch59 uses the next sentence 1.19.3); Ch54 historic present and BG 1.7.3.
- Kept out: *dōnec/quoad* as taught words, *dum* + indicative "until" (rare), *etsī/tametsī/etiamsī*, concessive *ut*, causal/concessive *quī* (§535e), *dignus quī*, *quam diū*, *priusquam* in general conditions (Off. 1.73), John 8:58 (cut, see L1 1), *est cum*.

## L1 (blocking): 3, all fixed
1. g-06 John 8:58 *antequam Abraham fieret, ego sum* was explained as "imperfect on the past team," but the main verb *sum* is present: the sequence claim was wrong (the imperfect follows the Greek, not Ch54's rule). Cut the frame, and the line from Speak-it-aloud.
2. Lectiō *Dum vīvō, canī nihil dabō* broke the chapter's own Law of the Four *Dum*s ("as long as" = same tense as the main verb; *dabō* is future). Now *Dum vīvam*, and the reading answer says why.
3. Sound check respelled *lūctūque* as "look-TOO-kwe": "look" gives a short vowel for long *ū*. Rewritten with an explicit long-*ū* note. Every other respelling re-derived from the penult rule (*antequam* AN-, *priusquam* pri-US- (s closes the syllable), *ōderint* OH-, *intersit* in-TER-, *adsēdistī* ad-say-DIS-, *maledīcere* ma-le-DEE-, *vīcēsimā* wee-KAY-).
