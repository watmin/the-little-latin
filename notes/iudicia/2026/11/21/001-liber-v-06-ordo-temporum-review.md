Decisions recorded:
- Terms: **sequence of tenses** ("the matching rule"; *ōrdō temporum*, with *cōnsecūtiō temporum* named as the Latin-grammar term), **primary sequence** ("the now team"), **secondary/historic sequence** ("the past team"), **historic present** (carded now; Ch52 named it in passing).
- Now team: present, future, future perfect, commands, present/perfect subjunctive main verbs, perfect = "have done". Past team: imperfect, perfect "did", pluperfect, imperfect/pluperfect subjunctive main verbs (A&G §482–483). *Utinam scīrem ubi esset* taught as "form picks the team".
- Goals never take perfect/pluperfect subjunctive. Tucked-in questions use same/earlier/later. After a now verb the perfect subjunctive covers did / was doing / has done (A&G §485b; Cicero *Laelius* 11 *quam … cārus fuerit … indicātum est*).
- "Have done" (A&G §485a): perfect normally secondary; primary only when clearly "have" and still true now (John 10:10, *Sen.* 84, *Ecce, cāseum ēmī ut edāmus*). *Iam rogāvī quid faciās* given as the rarer option for Ch53's *rogāvī*.
- Historic present: either team (A&G §485e): *BG* 1.7.3 *mittunt … quī dīcerent* vs *BG* 1.15.1 *praemittit, quī videant quās in partēs hostēs iter faciant*; *BG* 5.47.4 *remittit … ēductūrus esset* doubles as the "later in a past story" example. Learner's rule of thumb: present story → present subjunctives.
- Free result (A&G §485c): Cicero *Brutus* 302 *Ārdēbat … sīc, ut … vīderim*; *tam bene dormīvit ut … stertuerit* (and *stertēret* also right).
- Story: day 22 (*Diē vīcēsimā secundā*): no cheese in the house; Grandma sends Grandpa and John to the forum (*nē cāseum in viā edat*); Grandpa tells the merchant (from Ch51) the story of day 21 in good sequence, slips into historic present (*ut omnia iterum videāmus*); merchant: *Tam bene nārrāvistī ut tibi parvum cāseum dōnem*; John carries the big cheese home; the small cheese is missing — Grandpa gave it to the dog *ut grātiās agerem*; Grandma kisses him (*Tam bonus fuistī ut … dem*), then *Nē tetigerīs!* for the big one. **New canon: day 22 a new big cheese is on the table; the merchant's small gift cheese went to the dog as a thank-you; Grandpa did not eat it.**

## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Wrong stress (g-16):** *stertuerim* given as STER-tu-e-rim; penult *-e-* is short, so the stress is on the antepenult *-tu-*. → ster-TU-e-rim.
2. **Broken admonition title:** `!!! success "The Law of \"Have Done\""` (STATE rule: backslashes render literally). → typographic quotes “Have Done”.
3. **False claim (g-01):** *Ōrdō temporum* called "the old Latin grammarians' kind of name"; it isn't an ancient technical term. → "a Latin name for the same rule," plus the real Latin-grammar term *cōnsecūtiō temporum*.
