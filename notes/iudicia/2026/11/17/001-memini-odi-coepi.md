Decisions recorded:
- Terms: **defective verb** ("a verb with missing parts"; *dēficiō*; English *can* as the parallel), **genitive of the charge** ("the 'of what crime' genitive"), **impersonal verb of feeling** ("a feeling 'it' verb"; extends Ch44's Law of the Empty "It").
- *Meminī* and *ōdī* are taught as perfect-only verbs whose tenses slide one step (perfect = now, pluperfect = then, future perfect = later); *nōvī* (Ch21 *nōscō*, Ch40 *cognōscō*) joins them in meaning without being defective. *Coepī* is the "odd one out": a true past whose present side is borrowed from *incipiō*; *coeptus est* with a passive infinitive; *coepta* as a noun (Aen. 9.625; *Annuit coeptīs* on the dollar bill). *Mementō* only recognized (Ch39 *Aequam mementō*; Ch47 owns the form).
- *Inquit*: *inquam, inquis, inquit, inquiunt*; never first, slips in after the first word or phrase; quotes exact words only. *Aiō*: *aiō, ais, ait, aiunt*, "say yes" vs Ch42 *negō*; *ait* may precede the quote; *aiunt* + acc. + inf.; *Quid ais?* (Ch42 L3 *ut aiunt* stays out: subjunctive-free *aiunt* only.) Ch42 L3 Ennius oracle *Aiō tē, Aeacidā, Rōmānōs vincere posse* now paid as the two-accusative ambiguity.
- Genitive: remember/forget (person usually gen., thing gen. or acc.; Ch21 *meī/tuī* get a real job), *misereor* (Ps 50 *Miserēre meī*; church dative *miserēre nōbīs* flagged), charge (*accūsō, damnō, absolvō* + *fūrtī*), feeling "it" verbs (feeler acc., cause gen. or inf.; *piget* mentioned), adjectives (*cupidus, perītus, plēnus* (Ch10 Catullus paid), *memor/immemor*, *similis* (Ch44) in the Law; Horace *Integer vītae* as poetic "in respect of").
- Three Laws: **the Perfect That Means Now**, **the Slipped-In *Inquit*** (with *aiō* forms), **the Genitive's Grip**.
- Storyline: *diē quārtā decimā*, the walk to Rome (Ch45 hook). *Ambulāre coepimus*, Grandpa *Cāseī semper meminī*, Dad *Cantāre incipiam / Nōn licet!*, *"Ōdī," inquit Pater, "nōn cantāre!"*, Grandpa *Hanc viam nōvī!* and the kids' *Nōs taedet fābulārum*, *reī mīlitāris perītissimus*, *Cāseī cupidī sumus*, Grandpa forgets his Ch45 promise (*Nōn meminī*), the dog followed them from home (*Canis cāseī meminit*), Grandpa naps at the second milestone, *Pondus minus est*, at Rome the pack is empty, *Oblītus sum / Cāseī numquam immemor es*, Quintus *tē fūrtī accūsō*, Grandpa's Peter parody *Hunc cāseum nōn nōvī!*, refrain *In stomachō… canis! ## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Wrong grammar claim (intro, Law of the Perfect That Means Now):** *nōvī* presented as a perfect-only (defective) verb alongside *meminī* and *ōdī*, but *nōscō* has a full present system (and Ch21 taught it). → Intro and Law now say *meminī* and *ōdī* live only in the perfect, and *nōvī* (from *nōscō*) uses its perfect the same way.
2. **Wrong fact about a quoted line (g-04):** Matthew 26:72 framed as Peter's third denial "by the fire." In Matthew 26:72 it is the second denial (26:69–70 first, 26:73–74 third), and Matthew has Peter sitting in the courtyard (*foris in atriō*), not at a fire. → "waiting in the high priest's courtyard, is recognized a second time."
3. **Cards build failure:** vocab `la` values `ōdī, ōdisse (fut. part. *ōsūrus*)`, `coepta, coeptōrum (n. pl.)`, `fūrtum, fūrtī (n.)` do not match the Vocābula italic text (the hook compares only the first `*…*` span). → `la` trimmed; the parentheticals moved to `en`.
