Decisions recorded:
- Terms: **present imperative** ("the 'now' command"; Ch6's *amā, amāte* named), **future imperative** ("the 'shall' command"; old-school "second imperative"), **prohibition** ("a 'don't' command"; also "negative command").
- Forms: infinitive stem + *-tō* (third family *-itō*, no Ch6 clipping: *dīcitō, dūcitō, facitō*, but *fertō*); y'all *-tōte*; "they" form + *-ō* (*amantō, dūcuntō, audiuntō, capiuntō*); 2nd and 3rd singular share *-tō* (context decides); rebels *estō, estōte, suntō*; *ītō, ītōte, euntō* (Ch32 stem rule); *fertō, feruntō*; *mementō, mementōte* (Ch39/Ch46 paid); *scītō, scītōte*. *Nōlītō*, *-tor* passive/deponent future imperatives and *edō*'s *ēstō* deliberately left out (L3).
- Uses: **Law of Later and Forever** (present = now, to the person in front of you; future = later/always: after "if/when," laws, recipes, wills, letters; *mementō, scītō, estō* as the usual commands).
- Don'ts: **Law of the Five Don'ts**: *nōlī/nōlīte* + inf. (talk; "use this one"), *nē* + future imperative (laws, rules, recipes; *nēve/neu* for the second), *nē* + present imperative (poetry), *nōn* + future indicative (Ten Commandments), *nē* + subjunctive (*Nē timeās, Nē timuerītis*) as labeled chunks for Liber V. *Nōn* = the "not" of facts; *nē* = the "not" of don'ts and wishes (Ch34 *Nē quid nimis* explained).
- Storyline: *diē quīntā decimā*, the family walks home from Rome; Grandma writes six house laws *Duodecim Tabulīs similēs* (*Cāseum nēmō tangitō · Canis forīs manētō · Avus cāseō nē appropinquātō · Pater nē cantātō · Līberī cāseum custōdiuntō · Quī cāseum ēderit, sine cēnā dormītō*), *salūs cāseī suprēma lēx estō*, the laws work overnight, Grandma bakes Cato's *lībum* from the cheese, Grandpa eats it on a technicality (*hoc lībum est, nōn cāseus!*, *Avus iūris perītus est*), refrain *In stomachō… Avī!*, Grandma counters with law six (*ē cāseō factum est*), *Dūra lēx, sed lēx*, Dad asks to sing, *Nē cantātō!* New canon: Grandma's house laws exist (seven, counting *Lībum quoque nēmō tangitō*); the second cheese is gone (baked into the *lībum* and eaten by Grandpa); Grandpa went to bed without dinner.
