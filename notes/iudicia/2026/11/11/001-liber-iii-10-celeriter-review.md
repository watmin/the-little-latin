## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Wrong history claim:** g-03 said "*Bene* even changed its vowel (*bon-* → *ben-*)." Both forms go back to Old Latin *duenos/duenē*; it's *bonus* that changed, not *bene*. → "*Bene* even has a different vowel from *bonus* (both grew from an older *duenos*)."
2. **Doubtful Latin:** Lectiō *Marce, quōmodo es?* for "how are you?" is not idiomatic. → *Marce, quōmodo valēs?* (Ch1 *valeō*), English "how are you doing?"; g-13 frame updated.
3. **Doubtful Latin:** g-06 *Grātiās valdē!* for "thank you very much" (not a classical or standard phrase; Latin says *grātiās maximās*). → *Cāseum valdē amō!*, which also completes the verb/adjective/adverb trio from §1.
4. **Mistranslation against a taught distinction:** Lectiō *Sed celerrimē currimus!* glossed "as fast as we can," which is *quam celerrimē* (taught in §9). → "But we're running very fast!"
5. **Cards:** error card *Cāseus bene est* → "*Cāseus bonus est*" fixed Latin that is not clearly wrong (*bene est* "it's fine" is idiomatic). → replaced with *Lentiē, quaesō!* → *Lentius, quaesō!* (non-word; ref g-08).
