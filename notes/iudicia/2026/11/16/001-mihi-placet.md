Decisions recorded:
- Terms: **compound verb** ("a verb with a preposition glued on front"), **impersonal verb** ("an 'it' verb": no person as subject; *in-* + *persōna*), **dative of reference** ("the 'as far as X is concerned' dative"; older "dative of advantage/disadvantage"), **dative of purpose** ("the 'serves as' dative"; with a person = **double dative**; older "predicate dative"). Ch11's "to/for" case is the base.
- Three Laws: **Hidden "To"** (verbs, compounds, adjectives that mean "be ___ to" take the dative; English-thinks vs Latin-thinks table), **Empty "It"** (impersonals; *placet/licet/libet* + dative, *oportet/decet* + accusative; perfects *placuit, licuit, oportuit, libuit, decuit*; *placeō* also personal), **Two Datives** (purpose + reference; *cūrae, auxiliō, bonō, exemplō*). Compounds in an info table (A&G §370 prefixes).
- *Persuādeō* "make it agreeable to" (Wheelock's gloss); *nūbō* "veil oneself for" (L&S); *imperō/persuādeō* + what to do = Liber V (subjunctive), flagged in g-03. *Crēdō tibi* vs Ch42 *Crēdō tē venīre*. *Licet* + dative, *oportet* + accusative contrasted in g-12/g-13.
- Storyline: *diē duodecimā*, the day after Ch43 (dog ate the cheese). Grandma puts a new cheese *mēnsae impōnit*; *canī placuit, sed canī nocuit*; *Stomachus eī dolet*; *Quis cāseō praeerit?*; Grandpa *cāseō fidēlis* vs Cornelia's *inimīcus*; family vote *Placetne vōbīs? / Nōn placet!*; the dog becomes guard (*mēnsae obstā; nēminī licet cāseō appropinquāre*); Grandpa bribes (*mihi favē, persuāsī tibi?*), the dog *Aviae, nōn Avō, pāret*; *Spectāre licet; edere nōn licet*; Dad's *Licetne mihi cantāre? / Nōn licet!* gag; kids plead (*Avus nōbīs cūrae est; ignōsce eī*); Grandma forgives, gives Grandpa a piece; Grandpa offers the dog some, the dog refuses (*Aviae pāret, nōn stomachō*), *Exemplō mihi es!*; *In stomachō… Avī! Sed hodiē licuit.*

## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Subjunctive in a motto (Homework):** "*Hoc tibi cūrae sit… immō, cūrae est!*": *sit* is present subjunctive (Liber V keep-out). → "*Hoc tibi cūrae est!* This is your concern!"
2. **Wrong grammar claim (g-07 frame 2 + Law of the Empty "It"):** "an impersonal verb… only the 'it' form" contradicted section 2, where *placeō* is an ordinary verb with a subject (*Cāseus mihi placet*; *placēs* exists). → *placeō* can be personal or impersonal (with an infinitive or alone); *licet* and *oportet* are "it" verbs only; Law reworded ("used in the 'it' form only… *placeō* also has ordinary forms"); matching card (iv44-f-29) reworded.

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Wrong cross-reference (g-05 Ovid gloss):** "*sērō*: too late, Chapter 43": taught in Ch27 (Ch43 only reused it in the Lectiō). → Chapter 27.
2. **Wrong cross-reference (g-05 *impōnō*):** "Chapter 7's *in mēnsā pōnit*": *pōnō* is Ch31 (Ch7 has *in* + ablative only). → "Chapter 31's *pōnō* with *in* + ablative says the same."

6. **Inconsistent notation (Law of the Hidden "To" table):** the "Latin thinks" column switched prepositions ("Be easy **on** the cheese," "stands in front **of** the kitchen") in a Law about a hidden "to." → "Be lenient **to** the cheese"; *praesum* row says the prefix *prae-* brings the dative.
