Decisions recorded:
- Terms: **place where / place to which / place from which** ("the *Ubi? / Quō? / Unde?* places"; very old "whither / whence"), **locative** recalled from Ch8 ("the 'at' case") and widened, **ablative of time within which** ("the 'within' ablative"), **accusative of extent** ("the 'how long, how far' accusative"; older "accusative of duration of time / extent of space"). Ch8's Law of Home and Rome and Ch22's Law of When are the base.
- Locative rule taught as "borrows a look": genitive-looking for singular 1st/2nd-family towns (*Rōmae, Corinthī*), ablative-looking for plural and 3rd-family towns (*Athēnīs, Carthāgine*/*Carthāginī*). Ch8's "plural towns like *Athēnae* work differently; later" promise paid; Ch16 *vesperī* revealed as a locative; Ch38 *mīlitiae caelestis* (genitive) vs locative *mīlitiae*.
- Three Laws: **Home and Rome, full edition** (towns, small islands (*Rhodī*, Ch19 *Hīc Rhodus*), *domus*, *rūs*; locative-only *humī, vesperī, mīlitiae, bellī*; countries take prepositions), **Point and the Stretch** (point/window = ablative; stretch of time or space = accusative), **Ago and Later** (*abhinc* + acc.; *ante/post* + acc.; *tribus diēbus post* with Ch35's degree of difference).
- Ch22's "duration … fuller treatment later (Book IV)" note paid in g-08. Age idiom *Quot annōs nātus es?* (Ch43 *nātus*) with a tens box (20–90; 1–10 and *centum* from Ch22).
- Storyline: *diē tertiā decimā*, the day after Ch44. Evening at home, dog guards the cheese *humī*; Dad's singing gag (*Vesperī cantō! / Nōn licet!*, Ch16 *vesperī cantābō*); Grandpa's war story: *abhinc quīnquāgintā annōs mīles*, *decem annōs mīlitiae*, 20 then and 70 now; *Rōmae, Corinthī, Athēnīs, Carthāgine: ubīque!*; *Rōmā Brundisium … trecenta mīlia passuum*, *Brundisiō Athēnās nāvigāvimus*, *decem diēbus*, *cotīdiē trīgintā mīlia passuum* (sums check out); *trēs hōrās nārrat*, dog and kids fall asleep; Grandpa guarded the legion's cheese, "the enemy" ate it; Grandma *domī mīlitiaeque cāseum edis!*; Grandpa's Horace parody *Rōmae rūs optābam, rūrī Rōmam*; the cheese is missing; refrain twisted: *In stomachō… nūllō!* It's in Grandpa's *sarcina*, taken *cane dormiente*; *Crās Rōmam ībimus* (five miles, *Mīlitī nihil est!*); Dad *in itinere cantābō* / *Nōn licet!*. New canon: Grandpa is 70 and served ten years; Marcus 10, Cornelia 8 (g-09 only); the cheese is in Grandpa's pack and the family goes to Rome tomorrow (Ch46 hook).

## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Broken Latin in a trap frame (g-13 frame 5):** "*Lūcās dē mīlitiae caelestis …*": *dē* + genitive is wrong Latin and the quote is not Luke's. → "Chapter 38's *multitūdō mīlitiae caelestis*" (the real Luke 2:13 phrase).
2. **Wrong grammar claim (Law of Home and Rome, full edition):** "five little words (*domus, rūs, humus, mīlitia, bellum*) use the bare case" for to/at/from: *mīlitia* and *bellum* survive only as locatives (*mīlitiae, bellī*), not as bare "to/from" cases. → Law lists towns, small islands, *domus*, *rūs* for all three; "A few more words keep just a locative: *humī, vesperī, mīlitiae, bellī*." Card iv45-f-13 matches.

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Wrong etymology (g-10):** "English *mile* comes from *mīlle*": it comes from the plural *mīlia* (passuum). → fixed.
2. **Wrong cross-reference (g-05):** *lītus* "Chapter 16": taught in Ch15 (Ch16 reused it). → Chapter 15.
3. **Wrong cross-reference (Lectiō intro):** *ubīque* "Chapter 28": first taught in Ch8's motto *Nusquam est quī ubīque est*. → Chapter 8.
4. **Untaught word (g-12):** *Hōrā sextā cēnāmus / Quandō cēnātis?*: *cēnō* never taught. → *Hōrā sextā edimus / Quandō editis?*
5. **Unsupported Law item:** "small islands" stated with no example. → Ch19's *Rhodus* (*Rhodī*, at Rhodes) added in the Law.
6. **Overclaim (g-04):** *mīlitiae, bellī* "almost always come paired with *domī*." → "you'll usually meet them paired with *domī*."
7. **Over-general rule (g-08):** "A Latin present + a stretch of time = English 'have been ___ing'" holds only when the action is still going on. → condition added ("she's still at it").
8. **Inconsistent notation (g-16):** *domī* as DO-mee vs Ch8's DOM-ee; *mīlitiae* "-ye" vs the book's *ae* = "eye/mye" spellings (ROH-mye, KOO-rye). → DOM-ee, HUM-ee, mee-LI-ti-eye, mee-li-ti-EYE-kweh.
9. **Missed payoff (g-07):** *paucī* glossed as new; Ch22 taught it (partitive frame). → "Chapter 22's *paucī*", removed from the glossed list.
10. **Cards (choose iv45-c-abhinc-tres-annos):** distractor *abhinc tribus annīs post* was made-up Latin. → *tribus annīs* ("within three years"), with the why line explaining both distractors.
