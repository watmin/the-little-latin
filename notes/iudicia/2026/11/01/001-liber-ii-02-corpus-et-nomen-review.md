## L1 (blocks)
1. **Wrong grammar claim (absolute):** g-07 and the Law of the Disguise said *-us* with gen. *-oris/-eris* "means" neuter. Not always: *Venus, Veneris* f., *lepus, leporis* m. Now "almost always", with *Venus, Veneris* named as the famous exception (meaning wins, consistent with Ch13's gender-by-meaning frame).

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Law box wording false as written:** "It never becomes *-um*, *-ī*, or *-ō*" — but the dative is *corporī*. Now "It never turns into *corpum* or *corpō*."
2. **Unverified claim:** *Rōma caput mundī* "Medieval pilgrims sang it." Replaced with the verifiable "Medieval emperors stamped it on their seals" (Conrad II's bull: *Roma caput mundi regit orbis frena rotundi*).
3. **Overclaim:** "*Tangite nāsum!* … Latin keeps it singular." Distributive singular is common but not obligatory. Now "One nose each, so Latin is happy with the singular; *nāsōs* works too."
4. **Unglossed word in Lectiō:** *pulchrum* (not taught in Book I). Added to the new-words line.
5. **Tense slip toward Ch16:** *Nōmen damus!* was glossed "We'll give him a name!" (future). Now "We're giving him a name!"
6. **Incomplete rule statement:** g-08 frame 1 said the adjective matches "gender and number"; now "gender, number, and case" (Ch3/Ch12 wording).
7. **Muddled frame:** *Ō tempora!* subject or object: "the meaning doesn't need you to know" was garbled. Now says you can't tell from the ending (neuter twins), that grammarians call it the object form used for exclaiming ("Oh, me!"), and the meaning is clear either way.
8. **Garbled pronunciation note:** sound check said "Church Latin says the same words with 'MOH-res'", which is unclear and not useful. Replaced with "Long ē is 'ay,' as always."
9. **Card count 66**, over the ~60 target and with duplicates of other cards. Cut `f-in-ore`, `f-nomina`, `f-in-capite`, `f-crura-dolent` (each covered by a choose/forms card or the chapter's drill). Now 62.
