## L1 (fixed)
1. *Cāse(um) ēst* was syllabified *cā-sēst* (g-12, Lectiō line 23, card): eliding *-um* leaves *cāse-*, so it is ***cā-se-ēst***, three syllables. → fixed everywhere ("three syllables, not four").
2. Timeline: the intro and g-01 said Grandma/Grandpa promised "two nights ago." Ch66's Lectiō is day 34 and today is day 35 ("the morning after Chapter 66"), so the promises were **yesterday**. → "Yesterday."
3. g-03 said Ovid's line ends with a short "*-cris*." In the line it scans *vo-luc-ris*: the last syllable is ***ris***. → "ends *vo-luc-ris*: its last syllable, *ris*, is short."
4. g-14 syllabified *Ōd(ī) et amō* as *ō-det-a-mō*; the *t* goes with the next vowel: ***ō-de-ta-mō***. → fixed (+ "the *t* slides onto *amō*").
5. Intro line §5 *Tredecim* lacked its macron. → *Trēdecim*.
6. Motto §11 and Lectiō line 6 used *Quis pēs?* as "which foot?" (adjectival use wants *quī*). → *Quī pēs?* / *Quī pēs est?*

## L2 (fixed)
1. "Beat" collided with Ch66, which called Quintilian's *tempora* "beats" (a long = two beats). Today "beat" = *ictus*. → g-02 now calls the time units **counts** ("because 'beat' gets a new job in Section 6"); card f-06 too.
2. g-07 frame 1 answer contained a visible self-correction ("*nō / Trō*… wait: …"). → clean answer: "Foot 3 is *nō Trō*, and *canō* ends after its first syllable, *nō*: inside the foot."
3. g-12 frame 1 answer contained a false start ("*a-wi-ya-ta…* no:"). → "said *A-vi-et-A-vus*: the last *a* of *Avia* goes."
4. Notation clash: bold was declared the beat mark but g-06 used bold for the stressed syllable (**KA**-nō). → stresses in CAPITALS only; the tip now says bold marks the beat "when a line is marked for the beat."
5. Lectiō line 16 wrote *Mōnstr' horrendum* with an apostrophe while the chapter's notation is parentheses. → *Mōnstr(um) horrendum*.
6. g-06 frame 2 nested bold inside italics (`***Ar**-ma…*`) rendered with stray asterisks. → plain text with bold beat syllables.
7. Card f-49 listed "Four" elisions but showed three items. → "*mult(um)* and *ill(e)* (line 3), *quoqu(e)* (line 5), *atqu(e)* (line 7)."
