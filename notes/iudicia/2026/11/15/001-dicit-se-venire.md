- Terms: **direct statement** (exact words in quotes; *ōrātiō rēcta*), **indirect statement** (reported speech; *ōrātiō oblīqua*, "indirect discourse"), **accusative and infinitive** ("the reported-speech pattern"; "subject accusative" named in passing), **verbs of saying and thinking** (saying, thinking, knowing, perceiving). - Three Laws: **Reported Word** (no "that"; subject → accusative; verb → infinitive; direct/reported table), **Borrowed Clock, reported edition** (same/before/after vs *dīcit* and *dīxit*), **Speaker's *Sē*** (Ch21 Looking Back reaching into reported speech; *sē/suus* = the sayer, *eum/eius* = someone else).
- *Negō* taught as Romans' preferred "say … not"; *dīcit … nōn* allowed but discouraged. *Quod/quia* "that" flagged as later (Vulgate) usage, not to copy. *Esse* omission shown (*Putō Avum fessum*, Terence). Acc. + inf. as subject of *est* shown once (Psalm 133 payoff, *Bonum est līberōs lūdere*). Participle vs acc. + inf. after perceiving (*Canem lātrantem / lātrāre audiō*).
