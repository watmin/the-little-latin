## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Wrong gloss contradicting the Law (g-01):** *Utinam cāseum habeāmus!* glossed "If only we had cheese," an English contrary-to-fact wish, right before the frame saying a present-subjunctive wish can still come true. → "I hope we get some cheese!"
2. **Bad Latin (g-12):** *Mē cāseum ēsse velim* (reflexive accusative subject with *velim*, unidiomatic and untaught). → *Cāseum ēsse velim.*
3. **Missing macron (g-05):** Livy's *rēgem create* → *creāte* (matches homework and cards).

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Wrong cross-reference (g-09):** accusative + infinitive is Chapter 42 (*Dīcit sē venīre*), not 38. Fixed.
2. **Wrong cross-reference (Lectiō intro):** *ēmptus* as "Chapter 38's participle": the perfect passive participle is Chapter 37 (*Laudātus*). Fixed.
3. **Tense creep in the *vellem* gloss (g-12):** "I would have liked" is a past-unreal sense. → "I'd like, if only I could."
4. **Over-attribution (g-11):** "Cicero often asks friends: *Velim mihi scrībās*" read as a verbatim quote. → "Cicero's letters often ask this way," motto stays "teaching line."
5. **Abstract/Vocābula mismatch:** abstract listed *aliquis* (Chapter 29) as a chapter word with no Vocābula row. Removed.
6. **Imprecise blurb:** "the first king of Rome after Romulus" for Livy 1.17.10 (the speech is the interrex's call to elect one). → "the election of Rome's second king."
7. **Mistranslation (Lectiō):** *Valeātis!* rendered "Have a good time!" → "Fare well, y'all!" (y'all gloss).
8. **Soft attribution (g-05):** "A Roman letter or goodbye might say *Valeās*" → "Romans could also say goodbye with *Valeās!*"
