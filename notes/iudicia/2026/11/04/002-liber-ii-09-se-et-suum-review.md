- Induction: Ch11's *ego, mē, mihi, mēcum* paid off → genitive *meī/tuī* → full singular chant → *nōs/vōs* with *nōbīscum/vōbīscum* (Ch7 glue extended) → *nostrum/nostrī* vs *noster* → *sē* (no nominative) with *lavō* (Ch4 *lavat*) → *Avus sē lavat* vs *Avus eum lavat* → *suus* vs Ch19 *eius* → **Law of Looking Back** → *ipse* (*ipsīus/-ī* like Ch19) vs *sē* → *Nōsce tē* (Cic. *Tusc.* 1.52; later *Nōsce tē ipsum*) → *Ipse dīxit* (Cic. *N.D.* 1.10) → *Suum cuique* (Cic. *Off.* 1.15; *cuique* chunk) → Bias twin *Omnia sua sēcum portat* as **made example**, not a second Bias quote → Lectiō cheese ownership gag.

## L1 (blocks)
1. **Wrong preposition form (Accuracy):** *Ab fēminā ipsā* — before a consonant Latin wants ***ā***, not *ab*. Fixed to *Ā fēminā ipsā*.
2. **Untaught word in Lectiō (Ordering):** *cāseus alius* — *alius* never taught. Fixed to *hic cāseus* (Ch19).

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Perfect tense leakage (Ordering/keep-out):** Lectiō had *vīcit, ēdit, Lāvī, dīxī, ēdistī*. Rewritten with imperfect/present/future (*vincēbat, edēbat, lavābō, dīcō, edis*); Grandpa’s *Lāvī… id est* joke cut.
2. **Duplicate Lectiō motto:** *Fortūna caeca est* was Caput XX’s Lectiō motto. Replaced with *Cāseus suus an eius?*
3. **Macron:** soap is *sāpō, sāpōnis* (Pliny), not *sapō*.
4. **Section motto before its grammar:** §5 (Looking Back) opened with *Ipse dīxit* before *ipse* is taught. Moved *Ipse dīxit* to §6; §5 motto is now *Marcus librum suum legit, nōn eius.*
5. **Untaught infinitive in motto:** §7 *Suum cuique tribuere* used *tribuere*. Replaced with the Bias teaching twin *Omnia sua sēcum portat.*
6. **False perfect in a teaching frame:** *Fēminam ipsam vīdī* offered then walked back. Now only *videō*.
7. ***Deinde* untaught:** Grandpa’s line → *Tum canem lavābō* (Ch16 *tum*).
8. **2nd-person *suum* slip:** Avia’s *Nōn suum!* after addressing Grandpa → *Nōn tuum!*
9. **Attribution honesty:** *Omnia sua sēcum portat* labeled a teaching twin, not Bias; *Nōsce tē ipsum* flagged as later Latin vs Cicero’s *nōsce tē*.
10. **Redundant g-05 frame** left by the motto move; replaced with a combined *sē… et canem suum* drill.
11. **§6 cross-reference:** "*today's motto*" for *Nōsce tē ipsum* → "§1's motto."
