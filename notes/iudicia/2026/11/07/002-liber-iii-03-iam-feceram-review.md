## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Misleading tense claim.** g-06 (and card `iii27-f-time-order`) put *edet, ēderat, edit, ēdit, ēderit* on one absolute timeline with *ēderit* "before" *edet*. The future perfect is relative to a chosen future moment, not to the simple future; the same flaw would place *ēderat* before *ēdit* absolutely. → Reframed as a sort into past / now / future, with "before another past/future moment" glosses; card rewritten to match.
