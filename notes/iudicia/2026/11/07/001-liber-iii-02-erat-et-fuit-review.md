## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Untaught verb in a Law box.** Draft Law of the Stage and the Step used *Avus surrēxit?* (*surgō*, never taught) and *clāmāvit* (*clāmō* not introduced until g-05). → Replaced with *Subitō canis lātrāvit. Avus in culīnam vēnit.*
2. **Wrong pronunciation claim.** Sound check gave *Trōes* as "TROH-ays" (long ē). *Trōes* (Greek Τρῶες) has a short e. → "TROH-es (short e: a Greek-style plural)".
3. **Wrong gloss of a taught word.** Lectiō glossed Quīntus's *Ēheu!* (Ch16 "oh no, alas") as "Hey!" → "Oh no!"
