Decisions recorded:
- New terms, each in plain words: **positive** ("the plain form"; *positus* "set down"), **comparative** ("the 'more' form"; *comparō*), **superlative** ("the 'most / very' form"; *superlātus* ← *superferō*, tied to Ch32's *lātum*), old-school **degrees of comparison**, **ablative of comparison** ("the 'than' ablative").
- Laws: **the Comparative** (genitive stem + *-ior/-ius*; declines like *rēx/corpus*, not i-stem: abl. *-e*, n. pl. *-a*, gen. pl. *-um*; *ō* long except in the two naming forms); **Quam and the Ablative** (*quam* + same case always works; ablative without *quam* when the first term is subject/object); **the Superlative** (*-issimus*; *-er* → naming form + *-rimus*; six *-ilis* → *-limus*; declines like *bonus*); **the Five Rebels** (*bonus/melior/optimus, malus/peior/pessimus, magnus/maior/maximus, parvus/minor/minimus, multus/plūs/plūrimus*).
- *Maior, peior* written without macrons (vowel short; syllable heavy by the doubled *i*: MAI-yor, PEI-yor), per Wiktionary's note (citing Nishimura 2011); explained in g-08/g-09.
- *Plūs* singular = neuter noun + partitive genitive (*plūs cāseī*); *plūrēs, plūra* plural adjective; *plūrium* flagged; *plūrimum cāseī* same pattern.
- *Magis* + adjective for *-eus/-ius* adjectives (*magis caeruleus*) as recognition; *Citius, altius, fortius* and *minimē/optimē* as signposts to Ch34 (adverbs), *multō maior* as a chunk for Ch35.
- Payoffs: Ch3 *optima* ("strongest form of *bona*") and *optimus* "best, very good"; Ch19 *Antīquus cāseus optimus est*; Ch27/Ch28 *plūs quam perfectum* ("comparisons, Chapter 33"); Ch32 *mālō… quam* and "*quam* in comparisons: Chapter 33"; Ch32 Lectiō (Grandpa's homemade cheese finally made); Ch13 Law of the Stem and *rēx* endings; Ch14 Law of the Disguise (*altius, altiōris* ~ *corpus, corporis*); Ch20 *fortis* contrast (i-stem vs not), *ācer/celer/facilis*; Ch22 Law of the Part (*omnium*, *hōrum*, *plūs cāseī*); Ch2 *minimē*, Ch1 *optimē*; Ch13 *Iōanne* (Matt. 11:11); Ch15 Caesar *Gallia est omnis dīvīsa* continued (*Hōrum omnium fortissimī sunt Belgae*); Ch31 *lūx*; Ch32 Catiline; Ch23 *fidēs, spēs*; Ch21 *tē* as ablative.
- Lectiō: Grandpa's cheese is finally made; John's forum cheese is bigger; the kids judge (*Melle dulcior!*, *Crustula meliōra sunt quam cāseī!*, Dad *cantor optimus* → *Vōx tua pessima est!*), Grandpa *Cāseus meus fortissimus omnium est!* (*Nāsus mihi dolet!*), John's wins, Grandpa crowns himself *Optimus Maximus*, Avia *…et stomachus maximus!*, dog eats the biggest cheese (*Cāseus maximus… in stomachō… Cāseī!*) but refuses Grandpa's (*Canis tē sapientior est!*), ends *Cāseus meus… in stomachō meō. Optimus!*
