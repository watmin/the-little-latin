Decisions recorded:
- New terms, each in plain words: **irregular verb** ("a rule-breaker verb"; old-school "anomalous"); **compound verb** ("a verb with a little word glued on the front"); **impersonal** ("no person as subject"; recognition only, via Vergil's *Ītur*).
- Laws: **Ī and E** (*eō*'s stem is *e-* before a vowel, *ī-* before a consonant, short in *it*; perfect *iī/īvī, itum* regular); **the Missing Vowel** (*ferō* = *legō* minus the short *e/i* before *s, t, r*: *fers, fert, fertis, ferre, fer, ferte*; passive *ferris, fertur*); **the Three Wishes** (*vol-* → *nōl-/māl-*; *v-* forms → *nōn* + form / *mā-* + form; commands only *nōlī, nōlīte*).
- *Fīō* taught as "become / be made / happen" and as the present-system passive of *faciō* (*fit / factum est*; dictionary *fīō, fierī, factus sum*); nominative after *fīō* like after *est*; *fit, fierī* short, *fīō, fīunt, fīēbat, fīet* long.
- Payoffs: Ch8's promise ("Chapter 32 takes this tiny verb apart"); Ch7's "think exit" (*Exit / Exeunt* stage directions); Ch6 *fer/ferte* and *Nōlī mē tangere*; Ch16 *ībam/ībō* and Horace *Ībam forte*; Ch17 rebel future; Ch18 *mālō* recognition finished (and the *mālō*/apple trap); Ch25 *iī, tulī*; Ch27 *afferō*; Ch28 *relātum* (relative = "carried back"); Ch7/Ch35 ablative = *ablātum* "carried away"; Ch30 passive (*feror, fertur*, *ā līberīs fertur*), Ch30 L3 on impersonal passive (*Ītur*, recognition); Ch31 *Fīat lūx* and *Factum est*; Ch9 Pater Noster continued (*Fīat voluntās tua*, with *voluntās* ← *vol-*); Ch14 *Cāseus ex lacte est* → *Ex lacte cāseus fit*; Ch9 vocative in the Lectiō (*Abī, Cāsee!*).
- Lectiō: forum trip, nobody wants to carry the heavy cheese, Grandpa *Domī manēre mālō* is secretly making cheese from milk (*Crās cāseus fīet!*), the dog drinks the milk (*Exit canis… cum lacte in stomachō!*), ends *Crās cāseus fīet… in stomachō… Cāseī!*, *Cāseum emptum mālō!*, Avia *Fīat!*.
