## L1 (blocks)
1. **Gloss contradicts the imperfect's meaning (Accuracy/Logic):** the Lectiō line *Audiēbāmus, Pater… audiēbāmus* was glossed "We heard you, Dad… we heard," a plain finished past, exactly what Ch16's L1 fix says the imperfect usually is *not* (film vs snapshot); the g-07 frame then said "we were hearing it." Gloss now "We were listening, Dad… oh, we were listening," and the frame says why (still going on, not a one-time "we heard"). Rechecked every past line in the Lectiō and frames: all scene/habit/ongoing (*faciēbātis, legēbam, docēbam, currēbāmus, dormiēbat, audiēbant, stertēbās, labōrābam, cantābam, dīcēbam* "kept saying", *manēbat*).

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Self-contradiction:** g-02 hinted "Romans didn't say *audībam*" and then admitted old poets did. Hint now "the everyday form isn't *audībam*," note says early Latin and poets sometimes have it.
2. **Wrong count:** "all five families" contradicted Ch6's four conjugations (with *capiō* as the in-between). Now "all four families and the in-between"; card id `f-five-imperfects` renamed `f-all-imperfects`.
3. **Law had a hole:** "ask the infinitive" sorted *-īre* into the *-am* future, but *eō* (*īre*) says *ībō*. The Law now lists *eō* and *sum* as rebels, and spells out long *ē* in *-ēre* vs short *e* in *-ere* (the only thing that separates *habēre* from *legere*).
4. **Time-word clash with Ch16's Law of the Clock:** Ch16's frames paired *hodiē* with the present; the Lectiō's *Quid hodiē faciēbātis?* and Grandpa's *Hodiē dormiēbam* use it with the imperfect. g-07 frame 1 now explains (it's evening; *hodiē* covers whatever part of today you mean).
5. **Homework trap that isn't wrong:** "*Audībunt* (They will hear)" is an attested early-Latin future form. Replaced with *Audiunt* (present-for-future), a more useful trap; a matching error card `e-audiunt`.
6. **Unglossed word in a heading:** §5 title *Habēs an legēs?* used *an* ("or") which the book never taught. Glossed in g-05 frame 1.
7. **Gloss mismatch:** *cāseum tibi emam* was glossed "I'm buying the cheese for you"; now "I'll buy," so the reverse drill rebuilds the future.
8. **Missed teaching moment carried from Ch16 L3:** Mother's *In viā librum legēs* is a future used as an order; the frame now names it and links Ch16's *Tacēbitis!* (card `f-leges-command`).
