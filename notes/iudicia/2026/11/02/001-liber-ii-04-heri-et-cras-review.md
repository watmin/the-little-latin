Decisions recorded:
- Terms defined in plain words: **tense** = the time of the verb (from *tempus*, via French; "nothing to do with feeling tense"), **present** (now), **imperfect** (*imperfectum*, "not finished": was …ing / used to / kept …ing), **future** (will), **tense sign** (the little mark between stem and person ending). Stress is called "stress (emphasis)" in the sound check.
- Induction order: blurb *cantō / cantābam / cantābō* → learner spots *-bam/-bō* → chunks cracked (*amābō tē*, *dabō*) → full imperfect chant, learner finds *-bā-* and that only "I" changes (*-m*, tied to Ch5's "old and stubborn" *sum*) → 2nd conj → future, learner finds "bō, bi, bi, bi, bi, bu" → Law of the Time Signs named only after both tenses are drilled.
- Imperfect vs perfect: the imperfect is explicitly *not* the usual "I sang (once, done)"; that is the perfect, later ("a film still running vs a snapshot"). The Lectiō's yesterday lines are therefore all scene/habit lines (*natābam, spectābam, sedēbam, portābat, lātrābat, erat*), and "used to" is shown with *saepe* + imperfect.
- *Sum*: *eram…* shares the imperfect endings with the past *a* but no *b*; *erō…* shares the future's "ō, i, i, i, i, u" tune. *Eō* included cheaply (*ībam, ībō* = *ī* + same signs, since the stem is *ī-*), with Horace's *Ībam forte Viā Sacrā*.
- *Dō*: *dabam, dabō* with short *da-* throughout (matches Ch11's *dare*); payoff of Ch11's motto *Dā mihi factum, dabō tibi iūs* and of *Dabō tibi* in the Lectiō.
- 3rd/4th/-iō futures fenced off with an explicit "*legābō*? No: Chapter 17" frame, a homework fix item, and an error card. No 4th-conj imperfect anywhere (story avoids *dormiēbam*; Grandpa *natābat* instead).
- Payoffs: *amābō tē* (Ch11 chunk), *portābō* (Ch12 L3: *Cāseum magnum tibi portō, ave!* → *portābō*), *Dabō tibi*, *Hodiē mihi, crās tibi* (fill-in *est / erit*, framed as "one way to fill it in"), Ch14's *pedem dare*, Ch8's Law of the Switch (*in nūbēs* "into" vs Ch15's *in nūbibus*), Ch11's dative of possession in the future (*pars cāseī līberīs erit*).
- Time words: *heri, hodiē, crās, nunc, tum, mox, semper, saepe, numquam, iam (nōn iam), māne, vesperī*; *hodiē, nunc, semper, saepe, crās* are review and are re-carded under ii16 so the time set quizzes together. No *diēs* analysis.
- Story *Heri et crās*: the morning after the sea; everyone remembers yesterday in the imperfect (Marcus *rēx piscium eram*, Grandpa used to swim as a boy, the gull *cāseum portābat… in nūbēs*), then plans tomorrow in the future (stay home, Dad works and will sing, Cornēlia will train Cāseus, Iōannēs will bring a cheese from Rome, Grandpa's *amābō tē… cāseum mihi dabis?*); the gull is back in the garden; gag ends *in stomachō… Avī!* (genitive of *avus*, contrasted with *avis*).

## L1 (blocks)
1. **Misleading grammar claim by omission (Accuracy):** the draft taught the imperfect as "was …ing / used to" and then told yesterday's story in it without ever saying that a single finished past act ("I sang," "the bird stole") is normally the perfect. A learner would take *cantābam* as Latin's plain past. Added a g-02 frame ("Can *cantābam* mean a plain 'I sang'? Usually not… the perfect, a later chapter… film vs snapshot") and a matching card (`ii16-f-perfect-note`); checked every Lectiō past line is descriptive/habitual.

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Non-Latin interjection:** *Ō, nōn!* ("Oh no!") in the Lectiō is an English calque. Now *Ēheu!*, glossed as the Lectiō's one new word.
2. **Wrong back-reference:** g-02 said *sum* was from Chapter 2; *sum* and its "old and stubborn" *-m* are Chapter 5. Fixed, and quoted Ch5's phrase.
3. **Wrong back-reference:** g-03 cited *habeō, habēre* to Chapter 4 (only *habet/habent* there); the pair and the *ē* family are Chapter 5. Fixed.
4. **Tense mismatch in the Lectiō intro blurb:** *Heri ad mare ībant* used the imperfect for a single completed trip, contradicting the new L1 frame. Now *Heri in lītore erant* (a state).
5. **Overstated reading of a chunk:** *Hodiē mihi est, crās tibi erit* was presented as *the* missing verbs; the tombstone is elliptical and usually glossed "today me, tomorrow you." Now "One way to fill it in."
6. **Card id with a macron** (`ii16-e-erās-es`): ids must be ASCII like every other deck. Renamed `ii16-e-eras-es`.
7. **Vocab card would fail the build:** the `-bi-` row's first italic span is *-bi-*, so the card `la` "-bi- (-bō, -bunt)" didn't match. Card `la` is now "-bi-", with *-bō, -bunt* moved to `en`.
8. **Unclear card prompt:** `ii16-c-heri` read "\"___ I was walking…\"" (a blank in the English). Now gives the full English, blank only in the Latin.
9. **Card quality:** dropped `ii16-e-amabom` (an invented non-form, *amābom*, that teaches nothing the *-m* card doesn't); deck back to 62.
