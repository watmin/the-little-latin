Decisions recorded:
- Terms: **direct question** ("asked straight out"), **indirect question** ("the tucked-in question"; old school "indirect/dependent question"), **perfect subjunctive** ("the *-erim* subjunctive"), **pluperfect subjunctive** ("the perfect infinitive + ending subjunctive"). Stress glossed as "emphasis (stress)" in the sound check.
- Indirect question: subjunctive even after "I know" (*Sciō cūr rīdeās*); present subjunctive also covers "later" (*Nescīmus quandō … expergīscātur*), with *-ūrus sim* as the sharp "later" (Horace's *quid sit futūrum*). *Num/-ne* = "whether" (no "no" expectation), *-ne … an* for "whether … or." *Utrum … an* and *nesciō an* ("perhaps") not taught.
- Time choice: Law of Now and Earlier (now/past × same/earlier/later); "have done" main verbs flagged as "can count as now; Chapter 54" (sequence of tenses not named).
- Perfect subjunctive vs future perfect: *-erim* vs *-erō*, long *ī* in *-erīs/-erīmus/-erītis*; "he/they" identical; Romans blurred the *i* themselves (noted).
- *Nē* + perfect subjunctive = classical "don't," no past meaning (Matt 10:26; Horace *Odes* 1.11 *nē quaesierīs*, *temptāris* = *temptāverīs*).
- *Utinam* + pluperfect = past wish too late (Exodus 16:3 *mortuī essēmus*); three-wish ladder *pluat / plueret / pluisset*.
- Story: day 21 (*Diē vīcēsimā prīmā*): the big cheese gone from the table; tucked-in questions all round; Grandpa innocent (*Tōtam noctem stertēbat*); Grandma hid it *nē Avus eum nocte ederet* and forgot where (*nesciō ubi eum posuerim*); wishes too late; Dad *Nē cantāverīs, Pater!*; the dog finds it in Grandpa's box (*Nēmō enim umquam capsam tuam aperit* → *Nēmō… praeter mē!*); *Canis sciēbat ubi cāseus esset … quis eum cēlāvisset*; Grandpa gets a piece, *Nē tōtum cāseum ēderīs! — Nē timuerīs!*; an hour later the cheese is *in stomachō Avī*. **New canon: on day 21 Grandma hid the big cheese in Grandpa's box; Grandpa, given one piece, had eaten the rest within the hour.**

## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Wrong vowel length breaks the chapter's own Law:** the perfect subjunctive was written with short *i* in *Nē cantāveris, Pater!* (g-15, Lectiō, g-17) and *temptāveris* (g-14), the future-perfect spelling. → *cantāverīs*, *temptāverīs*.
2. **Wrong stress in the sound check (g-18):** *cēlāvissēs* given as kay-lah-wis-SAYS; the penult *-vis-* is long by position. → kay-lah-WIS-says.

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Wrong cross-reference (g-01):** *Quid facis?* attributed to Chapter 2; it's Chapter 6 (Chapter 2 has *-ne/num*). → "Chapter 6's question."
2. **Wrong verse claim (g-11):** "A verse earlier, Jesus says *narrā quanta tibi fēcit Deus*": both clauses are in Luke 8:39. → "Earlier in the same verse," with a gloss.
3. **Line numbers (g-14):** "Line 3 goes on: *nec Babylōniōs temptāris numerōs*": the phrase straddles lines 2–3. → "(lines 2–3)"; *carpe diem* note reworded to "the poem's last line."
4. **Unexplained stem (g-14):** *quaesierīs* glossed with perfect *quaesīvī* only; the form is from *quaesiī*. → "*quaesīvī* or *quaesiī*."
5. **Loose time reference (g-10):** "as yesterday" for Ch52 (published the same morning). → "as in Chapter 52."
6. **Count mismatch (g-17):** "Two wishes too late" but the answer lists three. → "Three."
7. **Unglossed word (g-09):** *nostrum* in Cicero. → glossed "of us."
8. **Unglossed chunk (g-02):** *Quōmodo valēs?* not taught as a chunk. → glossed, *valeō* Chapter 50.
9. **Spoiler in the Lectiō intro:** "Last night Grandma took the big cheese off the table" gave away the twist. → "Last night the big cheese was sitting on the table."
10. **Overclaim (g-03):** "He answers the brothers" for Matt 20:22; the text says only *Respondēns autem Iēsus dīxit*. → "He answers them."
11. **Card with a phone-unfair distractor (c-05, cards draft):** "Don't be afraid!" offered *Nē timueris* beside *Nē timuerīs* (macron-only difference). → distractors *Nē timēbis! / Nē timuistī!*.
