Decisions recorded:
- Terms: **imperfect subjunctive** ("the 'infinitive + ending' subjunctive"; jobs today: past stories and "not really" wishes; name borrowed from Ch16's imperfect, doesn't mean "was …ing") and **result clause** ("the 'so … that' clause"; old-school "consecutive clause," *cōnsequor*). "Historic present" glossed in plain words in g-08 (not carded).
- Forms: present infinitive + *-m, -s, -t, -mus, -tis, -nt* (Law of the Infinitive Plus); long *ē* except before final *-m, -t, -nt* (Ch16 habit); *darem* short *a*; Ch49's *-im* rebels are regular here (*essem, possem, vellem, nōllem, māllem*); *īrem, ferrem*; *fierem* (pretend *fiere*); passive *amārer…*; deponents from a pretend active infinitive (*loquerer*); *expergīscerētur* in g-14. *Ēssem* (alt. of *ederem*) not mentioned.
- Wishes: *utinam* + present = may still come true (Ch50); + imperfect = not really so now (English "I wish you were"); *nē* kept for "not" wishes. Pluperfect wishes left for Ch53.
- Past goal: "have done" (*vēnī*) counts as now (consistent with Ch51's Law); past story → imperfect; Caesar's historic present flagged ("writers can go either way; Chapter 54"). Sequence of tenses not named.
- Result: subjunctive even when true (mood marks the clause hanging on "so," not "maybe"); signposts *tam, ita, sīc, tantus, tot*; present result after a now verb (*Tam fessus sum ut vix stāre possim*), and after a "have done" verb (Cicero *Sen.* 84). Negative = *ut nōn, ut nēmō, ut nihil, ut numquam, ut nūllus*; *nē* = wanted. Perfect subjunctive in result clauses not mentioned (Ch53+).
- Story: day 20 (*Diē vīcēsimā*); a storm in the night *tanta … ut nēmō dormīre posset*; Marcus *tam fessus ut vix stāre possim*; Cornelia studied *ut omnia scīrem* (Ch51 quiz pays); Grandpa asleep at the table; the small cheese gone from the cupboard (*nē quis eum tangeret*); Marcus/Quintus recount the forum (Ch51) in past goals; the dog cleared (*tam parvus ut armārium aperīre nōn possit*; Ch14 canon *Cāseus canis parvus est*); cheese in Grandpa's beard; *Quid facerem? Tanta erat tempestās…*; *Utinam iuvenis essem!*; Cornelia *tam bene respondī ut mē laudāret*, gets a piece of the big cheese; the dog licks Grandpa's beard; everyone laughs *ut canis quoque lātret*. **New canon: on the night of day 19/20 Grandpa ate the old small cheese; Cornelia was praised by her teacher on day 20.**

## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Wrong case in the Lectiō:** Grandma calls *Avus! Tū cāseum nostrum ēdistī!* Vocative of *avus* is ***Ave*** (book convention since Liber II: *Ave! Quid facis?*). → *Ave! Tū cāseum nostrum ēdistī!*
2. **Overclaimed grammar rule (g-08):** "It's still a past story, so the goal takes the past form" implies a historic present always takes the imperfect; writers use either. → "He knows it's past, so here he gives the goal the past form … (Writers can go either way after a historic present; Chapter 54 sorts it out.)"

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Wrong cross-reference (g-07):** *iī* attributed to Chapter 32; it was taught in Chapter 25 (Ch32 itself says "Chapter 25: … *iī, īstī, iit*"). → "Chapter 25."
2. **Unsupported paraphrase (g-07):** "Paulus goes on to ask who bears the risk if Stichus dies." The text says *periculo meo Stichus erit* (Stichus is at my risk); death isn't in the sentence. → "Paulus goes on to say who bears the risk for Stichus."
3. **Loose gloss (g-12):** Matt 8:28's *duo habentes daemonia* rendered "two wild men." → "two men possessed by demons."
4. **Motto attribution (§8):** *Lēgātōs mittunt quī dīcerent* is an abridgement of BG 1.7.3. → "(after Caesar, *Gallic War* 1.7.3)."
5. **Edition difference (g-11):** John 3:16 quoted from the Nova Vulgata (*dīlēxit Deus*); the Clementine has *Deus dīlēxit*. Added a note so a reader with a Clementine Bible isn't confused.
6. **Unexplained expansion (g-05):** Suetonius writes *p. R.*; the frame silently expanded it. → note "*p. R.*, short for *populus Rōmānus*."
7. **Clumsy Latin in the Lectiō:** *Cāseum … pōnō, ut omnēs eum videant, nē Avus eum sōlus edat* stacked two purpose clauses with no connective (classical would need *nēve*/restructure). → *Cāseum magnum in mēnsā pōnō, nē Avus eum sōlus edat*; g-16 question updated to match.
8. **Frame count over band (structure):** 83 frames > ~80. Trimmed *hortārētur* (g-04), *Quid dīcerēmus?* (g-06), *ēsuriēbāmus* (g-10), and folded g-09's "how do you spot it" frame into the term frame. 79 frames; matching cards removed.
9. **Card with two right answers (c-04):** "I shut the cheese away so the dog wouldn't eat it" offered *nē canis eum edat* as a distractor, which is defensible if *clausī* = "I have shut." → distractor *nē canis eum edēbat* (indicative, clearly wrong).
10. **Card build failure (vocab la):** gender markers "(f.)/(m.)" sit outside the italics in Vocābula, so `la` must omit them. → `la: tempestās, tempestātis` / `flūctus, flūctūs`, gender moved into `en` (Ch51 style: "kiss (n.)").
