## L1 (blocks)
1. **Wrong historical claim (Accuracy):** the *velle* frame said Roman *-lle/-sse* "started out as *-l-re, -s-re*." Backwards: the old infinitive ending was *-se* (*es-se* kept it; *vel-se* → *velle*), and *-re* is that *s* rhotacized between vowels. Rewritten that way, tied to Ch14's *corpos- → corpor-*.

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **False cross-reference:** g-04 said Chapter 12 "quoted a rhyme and stopped halfway." Ch12 quoted the whole rhyme (in a frame and in the motto gloss) but couldn't explain *vīs*. Now "quoted a whole rhyme but could only explain its first half… read the second half yourself."
2. **Misattributed speaker:** *Nōn omnia possumus omnēs* (Ecl. 8.63) was "Vergil's shepherd shrugs." The line is the poet turning to the Muses before handing over to Alphesiboeus. Fixed.
3. **Prompt/answer mismatch:** "I could run all day" vs *semper* ("always"). Prompt now "As a boy, I could always run," answer explains the past habit (Ch16's *Avus puer saepe natābat*).
4. **Sloppy homework line:** a translate item read "*Avus exspectāre nōlēbat… sorry, nōn vult*" (and *nōlēbat* isn't taught). Now *Avus exspectāre nōn vult.*
5. **Story logic in a frame:** g-07 said "Three kids, one verb in 'we'" for Marcus's *Simul portāre possumus*, which he says to Quīntus before Cornēlia joins. Now explains the two-then-three steps.
6. **Vague reference:** "Chapter 6's motto, Horace's *Sapere audē*": Ch6 has ten mottos. Now "Chapter 6's sound-check motto."
7. **Unverifiable specific:** "Plautus still sometimes says *potesse*" softened to "Early Latin sometimes still says *potesse*" (A&G-level claim, true).
8. **Over length:** 81 frames, above the rubric's ~40-80 and the Book II norm (~78).
