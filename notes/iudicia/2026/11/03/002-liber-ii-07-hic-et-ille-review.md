## L1 (blocks)
1. **Untaught grammar in the Lectiō (Ordering):** Grandpa's *Quid faciam?* is a deliberative present subjunctive (flagged as a Ch17 L3), and the frame told John to "read it as a future", which is wrong. Line rewritten (*Hic stomachus meus cāseum amat!*) and the frame cut.

## L2 (fixed before publishing)
1. **Forward reference treated as known:** the *huic* frame said "Guess from *cuius → cui*", but *cui* has never been taught (Ch10 had only *Cuius est?*; *cui* → Ch29). Now "Another surprise", with *cui* only signposted in the answer.
2. **Miscount in a citation:** *Dabit deus hīs quoque fīnem* (1.199) is "four lines" before *Forsan et haec* (1.203), not five. Fixed.
3. **Wrong cross-reference:** "Chapter 14's Law of the Neuter Twins": the Law is Chapter 4's (Ch14 restated it). Fixed.
4. **Overclaim in a Law:** the Law of the Pointing Words said *hic, ille, iste, is, īdem* all decline "almost like *magnus*" with three surprises; *hic* has more quirks (*hunc, haec*). Now *ille, iste, is, īdem* in the rule, with *hic* noted as sharing surprises 1-2.
5. **Inaccurate example in the same Law:** surprise 3 listed *idem* among neuters "ending in *-d*". It ends in *-m*. Now "(and *idem* is *id* + *-dem*)".
6. **Citation precision:** the *iste* motto cited "Valerius Maximus 8.7" and silently dropped *inquit*. Now "8.7 ext. 7; *inquit*, 'he said,' left out"; the frame quotes it with *inquit*.
7. **Inconsistent gloss:** Sallust's *firma amīcitia* was "true friendship" in the motto but "firm friendship" in the frame. Unified to "firm."
8. **Story logic:** the closing line *Īdem cāseus, sed nōn īdem stomachus!* contradicted Iōannēs's line just before (*Nōn eundem, Ave*: it's **not** the same cheese). Rewritten to *Nōn īdem stomachus… et hic stomachus meus vacuus est!*, and the frame updated.
9. **Unclear setting:** the Lectiō moves home → market → home with no stage cue. The English intro now says so.
10. **Over length:** 82 frames, above the Book II norm (~78). Cut the *Quid faciam* frame (L1), the duplicate "I'll give him cheese" drill in g-06 (g-08 has it), and merged "With this girl / in this garden" into the *hoc/hōc* frame. Now 79.
11. **Ambiguous vocab gloss:** *atque* "and (also *ac*)" could read as "also". Now "and (shorter form: *ac*)".

## L3 (logged)

- Prose *illīus/istīus* vs poetic *illĭus*; *alterius* etc.
- *Hic … ille* = "the latter … the former"; *ille* after a name = "that famous" (*Archimēdēs ille*, *Alexander ille*); *is quī* = "the one who" (with relatives, Ch29+).
