## L1 (blocks; fixed)
1. **Untaught word:** the Lectiō used *Cūr…?* twice (Marcus, Quīntus). *Cūr* has never been taught in Ch1–35 (checked with rg over docs/). → Ch35's chunk *Quā dē causā…?* both times (a nice payoff; Avus answers with Ch27's glossed *quia*).
2. **Story logic gap:** John said *Partem servāvī, quam in sacculō meō posueram*, but the only cheese in reach was hidden in Grandma's cupboard and Ch35's doctor carried John's cheese away; the piece came from nowhere. → *Medicus cāseum meum abstulit… sed partem iam servāveram! In sacculō meō est.* (pluperfect before Ch35's *abstulit*; Ch32 *auferō*); g-13 frame rewritten.
3. **Inaccurate history claim:** g-01 said Roman grammarians saw each case as the noun "falling away from its naming form." The nominative is itself a *cāsus* (*cāsus rēctus*), and *cāsus* translates Greek *ptōsis*. → "Roman grammarians copied the Greek word for a case, *ptōsis*, 'a falling': each case is one way a noun 'falls' (even the naming form, the 'upright' *cāsus rēctus*)."
