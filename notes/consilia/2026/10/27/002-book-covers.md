# Consilium: book covers (2026-10-27)

One cover per Liber, 3:4 JPEG, pen-and-ink on a cream background, a single red accent (the helmet crest). Title "The Little Latin" at top, "Liber N" at bottom.

**Mascot arc:** the same legionary ages across the six books.
- **Liber I:** a young recruit on a bench, writing on a wax tablet, with an eagle **chick**. Beside the bench: a **wooden practice sword** and a **wicker shield**.
- **Liber II–V:** in between. He gets older, the eagle grows, and the gear gets more real (to be drawn later, same style).
- **Liber VI:** a **grey-bearded veteran** reading a scroll, with an **adult eagle**. Beside the bench: a **worn scutum** and a **gladius**.

**On the site:** `docs/assets/covers/liber-<roman>.jpg` (720×960, about 100 KB). Each `docs/liber-<roman>/index.md` is a landing page: the cover (`<figure class="book-cover">`, max 360px, centered) above the chapter outline. Books without a cover yet show "Cover coming soon." To add one, drop in the JPEG and replace that line with the figure block, copying Liber I's alt-text style.

**Home hero** (`docs/assets/hero.jpg`, 16:9, 1600px wide at most): the legionary walks a Roman road toward an intact Colosseum, with the eagle flying alongside. The title "The Little Latin" is part of the image, so the page's H1 is visually hidden but kept for screen readers and search. The image spans the full content width.
