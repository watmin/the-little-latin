# The Little Latin

*Salvē!* This is a book for relearning Latin by **ear and mouth first**, one tiny step at a time.

## How to use this book

Each chapter is a long column of questions and answers.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What do I do with the left column? | Read the question and answer it, out loud if you can. |
| And the right column? | Cover it with your thumb. Peek only after you've guessed. |
| What if I guess wrong? | Good. That's how you learn. Keep going. |
| Should I hurry? | Never. Tiny steps. Have a snack. |

!!! tip "The rules"
    - Say every Latin word **aloud**. Latin was a spoken language before it was a homework assignment.
    - Long vowels wear a bar on top (a *macron*): ā ē ī ō ū ȳ. Hold them about twice as long.
    - When you see a **Law**, you probably already figured it out. The box just gives it a name.

## Pronunciation

We use **classical (restored)** pronunciation: the Latin of Cicero, said *KEE-ke-rō*.

## The books

- **Liber I: Prīma Verba** (First Words): sounds, greetings, simple sentences, the cases one by one
- **Liber II: Rēs Cotīdiānae** (Daily Things): more nouns, past and future, numbers and time
- **Liber III: Fābulae** (Stories): narrating the past, relative clauses, the passive
- **Liber IV: Verba Agentia** (Verbals): participles and infinitives, reported speech
- **Liber V: Modus Subiūnctīvus** (The Subjunctive): wishes, purposes, conditions
- **Liber VI: Ad Auctōrēs** (To the Authors): gerunds, poetry, real Roman texts
